MEET RENÉE

A creative life shaped by stories, music and meaning.

I have never followed just one creative path. I’m an author, singer and memorial-jewellery designer—three very different forms of expression, yet each rooted in storytelling, memory and human connection.

Born into an Army family and raised between England, Germany and Guernsey, I later spent many years in Jersey before making my home in Spain. Along the way, I worked in the legal world, played music, wrote stories and discovered the deeply personal art of creating jewellery that holds the memory of someone loved.

Today, my life brings all of those threads together: writing books inspired by remarkable places, performing music that brings people together, and creating bespoke memorial pieces with care and respect.

Behind it all, I’m married to Alan, mum to our two adult children, Thomas and Katie, and thoroughly supervised by our two dogs, Poppy and Maia.
