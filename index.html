ABOUT RENÉE

A creative life shaped by stories, music and meaning.

Renée Wallwork is an author, singer and designer whose work moves between history, memory and human connection. Whether through the atmosphere of an old building, the intimacy of a memorial piece, or the energy of a live performance, her work is rooted in the things people carry with them.
